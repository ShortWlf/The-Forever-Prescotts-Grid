Join Us On The Water

To visit the memorial and participate in grid fishing events, add our login URI to your preferred OpenSimulator viewer (such as Firestorm OpenSim Edition).

http://rff.ddns.net:8002
  1. Open your viewer's Preferences (Ctrl + P) and choose the Grids tab.
  2. Paste the login URI above into the "Add New Grid" field and click Apply.
  3. Select Forever Prescotts from your grid selection drop-down on the main login screen.
  4. Enter your account credentials and set your home or login location to Reel Fun Fishing.
